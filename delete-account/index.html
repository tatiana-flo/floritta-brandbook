Delete your Floritta account

This page explains how to delete your account and personal data for the Floritta – Flower Delivery app and floritta.uk. Developer: Floritta Ltd, Unit 3 The Willows, 80 Willow Walk, London SE1 5SY.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app: open You → Privacy → Delete my account. This opens an email to us with your request already written.
  2. Or email info@floritta.uk with the subject “Delete my account”, from the email address on your account (or tell us the phone number you order with).

We may ask you to confirm it is you. We complete the request within one month and email you when it is done. There is no charge.

What we delete

  • Your account and login details, saved addresses and recipients, saved card message drafts and preferences.
  • Your loyalty points, marketing choices and chat history with Flora and our team in the app.
  • Details saved on your phone by the app are removed when you delete the app or tap “Forget my details” in You.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Records of orders, payments, refunds and invoices – 6 years after the end of the financial year of the order, because UK tax law requires it. They are kept only for that purpose.
  • A minimal “do not contact” entry if you asked us to stop marketing, so we can keep that promise.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
