FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this only for flower shops?

No. It started in a florist, but the same core – orders, inventory with recipes, customers, finance, team – runs cafés, bakeries, restaurants and specialty retail. Presets tailor it to your trade.

Do we have to replace our till?

No. Many teams run Floritta One alongside their till for orders, stock, customers and finance, and consolidate later.

How long does it take to go live?

Most single locations are live within days. Guided import with a dry-run preview brings your products, customers and stock across, and we map it with you.

What happens to our existing data?

You bring it with you: products, customers and stock by import. It stays yours.

Does the AI send messages on its own?

Never. It drafts; a person on your team reads and presses Send. AI drafts run on your own AI API key, so usage is billed to you directly by the AI provider, not included in the plan price.

Which languages are supported?

English and Russian are built in and switch per person. More on request.

What happens after I book a demo?

We reply within one business day, agree a time, and spend 20 minutes showing the system around your trade. No card, no commitment.
