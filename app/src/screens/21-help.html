How can we help?

Common questions
Where and when do you deliver?
Across London, seven days a week, 8:00–22:00. Choose today or a later date and a delivery window at checkout. Delivery is free on orders over £120, otherwise £7.95.
What is the same-day cut-off?
Same-day delivery across London is available until 10 pm. Order before then, pick a window that is still open today, and we hand-tie and deliver it the same day.
Will I see the bouquet before it's delivered?
Yes. Your florist photographs the finished bouquet before it leaves the shop, so you know exactly what arrives.
Refunds and replacements
Flowers are perishable, so they can't be returned. If anything arrives damaged or incorrect, send us photos within 24 hours of delivery and we'll put it right. Our flowers are guaranteed fresh for at least 3 days – if they wilt sooner, send a photo and we'll replace the bouquet.
Are your flowers pet-friendly?
Bouquets marked “Pet-friendly” contain no lilies or other stems known to be toxic to cats and dogs. Not sure? Message us before you order.
