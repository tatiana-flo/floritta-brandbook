Not in this build
  • No server moderation: reports stay on the phone and reach nobody at Floritta – a reported post is simply hidden for this user.
  • Reported posts cannot be brought back; «Show hidden posts again» restores hidden ones only.
  • No camera in «Post a bouquet» – gallery only; the photo is kept as a local file path.
  • No custom hashtags – nine fixed chips. «Follow tag» changes nothing in the feed.
  • Own comments cannot be deleted or edited; deleting a post removes its comments, like and bookmark with it.
  • Other people never see your posts, likes, follows or comments – the whole community is local to the device.
  • «Forget my details on this phone» on the You tab does not clear the community store.
  • Pull-to-refresh only animates; «Load more» appends six fixed posts once.
All members, posts and comments by others are examples – the app says so in the header, on the banner and above every post.
