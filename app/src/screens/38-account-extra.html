Where you find them

Tile “Orders” on You (with the count or “None yet”), “Track order” after payment, and the empty Keep it alive screen.

Bouquet builder on floritta.uk

A bouquet built and paid on the website inside the app is saved too – as “Your own bouquet (N stems)” or “Floritta order”. It has no product card, recipient or date, so Reorder is not available. If the order number cannot be read from the website, it is kept under a short code like web-3f9a1c2d – Track still works.

Not here

Orders placed on floritta.uk in Safari, by phone or WhatsApp, and orders from another phone or before a reinstall – they are not synced to the app.
