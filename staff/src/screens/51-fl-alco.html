Six rules

Challenge 25
If the recipient looks under 25, ask for ID: passport, photo driving licence or PASS card.
No ID – no alcohol
Don't hand it over. Report a problem and bring it back – you're paid the full rate plus 50%.
Not to someone drunk, not for a minor
If they seem drunk or are clearly buying for someone under 18, refuse.
Sealed, as packed
The bottle stays sealed in the gift. Never open or decant.
Licensing hours
Alcohol is handed over only within the studio's licensed hours – the slot already respects them.
Licence 889934 · condition 342
Floritta's premises licence requires a trained courier on every 18+ delivery. Training is valid for 6 months.
